# R.A.I. Model Limitations & National Scaling Roadmap

## 1. Current Limitations

1. **Micro-Scale Topography**: Hyper-local mountain convective cloudbursts in deep Himalayan valleys require micro-barometer station density (<2 km grid).
2. **Satellite Pass Latency**: NASA GPM IMERG Early Run latency is ~4 hours; operational real-time reliance uses high-resolution numerical forecasts for immediate 0–3h nowcasting.
3. **Disaster Warnings Authority**: R.A.I. outputs algorithmic probability estimates and does not replace statutory disaster alerts issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD) or NDMA.

---

## 2. National Scaling Roadmap

1. **Nationwide Grid Ingestion**: Expand ingestion grid from 10 representative stations to all 700+ IMD district weather stations across India.
2. **Doppler Radar (DWR) Integration**: Ingest live radial velocity and reflectivity arrays from IMD's 37 national Doppler Weather Radars.
3. **Hyperlocal Soil Moisture Sensing**: Ingest ISRO EOS-04 C-band radar and ground IoT probes to enhance agricultural flood and waterlogging alerts.
